One of the most useful things Teach Anything gives you is a window into what your students are actually asking. Every conversation with your assistant is saved in an anonymized form so you can review it later.

## What you can see

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The full exchange" icon="messages-square">
    Read each question a student asked and exactly how the assistant answered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The sources used" icon="quote">
    See which of your materials the assistant drew on for each answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search and sort" icon="search">
    Look for a topic, a keyword, or sort by most recent to find what you need.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Browse by page" icon="list">
    Page through the history when there's a lot of activity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Why it's worth your time

Reading conversations tells you things a syllabus can't:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Spot common confusion">
    If ten students ask the same thing, that's a topic to revisit in class or
    add to your materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Catch gaps in your materials">
    If the assistant often says "that isn't in the course materials," you've
    found something worth uploading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check answer quality">
    Skim answers to make sure the tone and accuracy match what you want. If they
    don't, adjust the
    [instructions](/instructors/customize#the-instructions-field).
  </Step>
</Steps>

:::tip[Turn insight into action]
Found a recurring question? Upload a short document that answers it directly, or fold the answer into your assistant's [instructions](/instructors/customize#the-instructions-field), so it's always ready.
:::

## Quiz results show up here too

When a student asks the assistant to quiz them, the quiz shows up right inside their conversation, so you can see how they did while you review.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The questions asked" icon="list-checks">
    See the multiple-choice questions the assistant generated from your
    materials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What the student picked" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    Each question shows the answer they chose and whether it was right.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Their score" icon="target">
    See how many they got right out of the total.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every attempt" icon="repeat">
    If a student retook the quiz, each attempt is recorded.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Quiz results are also included when you [export chat records](/instructors/export-chat-records), so you can pull them alongside the rest of the conversation.

## Student ratings show up here too

Students can give any answer a thumbs up or a thumbs down, from the shared link or the chat button on your site. A rating is the student's own impression of an answer, not a measure of whether it was correct: a sound answer can still get a thumbs down, and that gap is worth knowing about.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="On each answer" icon="thumbs-up">
    Open a chat and every rated answer shows the thumb the student gave it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On each chat" icon="list">
    The list of chats shows how many thumbs up and thumbs down each one got.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find the thumbs down" icon="thumbs-down">
    Turn on **Has thumbs down** to see only the chats where a student rated an
    answer thumbs down.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The overall picture" icon="target">
    The **Analytics** tab shows the share of ratings that were thumbs up, the
    total, and the trend by day over the last 30 days.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Ratings are only collected from students. Your own test chats in the **Chat** tab have no rating buttons, so your testing never mixes into the numbers. Ratings are shown to you alone and are never used to train an AI model.

:::note[What students are told]
Feedback and performance ratings submitted by students regarding the AI chatbot are collected anonymously to improve educational quality. Do not include sensitive personal information in your written feedback.
:::

Ratings are also included when you [export chat records](/instructors/export-chat-records).

## A note on student privacy

Conversations are visible in an anonymized form to you as the instructor so you can improve your course. Let your students know their questions may be reviewed anonymously, and treat what you read with the same care you'd give any student communication.
